An exercise from the "Marketing stack and no-code tools" course: build a landing page that automates lead capture, from the visitor's input to an already-sorted database.

## The site

Vertige is a fictional 600 m² bouldering gym. The page sells a single offer, a €15 discovery session with gear included:

- a short, no-commitment form: first name, email, preferred day (from today) and time slot (morning, afternoon or evening), with a clear message for each error, for a failed submission and for the confirmation;
- the key arguments (beginner-friendly, no gear to buy, staff on hand);
- how the session works in three steps, the gym itself, and the membership offered afterwards;
- sample reviews, an FAQ and a final reminder of the offer.

## The automation

When the form is sent, an **n8n** workflow picks up the request and stores it in **Airtable**, already sorted, so the gym's team finds each sign-up in the right place without retyping anything.

## How it was built

The page was designed with Claude, then connected to n8n and Airtable. The content is a mockup: the gym, its figures and reviews are fictional.
