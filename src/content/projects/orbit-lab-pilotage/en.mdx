A control room for a fictional company, built on the July to September quarter of a course case study.

- **Overview**: ad spend, qualified leads, signatures and contracts, then the funnel from lead to contract and the three key takeaways for the period.
- **Acquisition**: Google Ads, LinkedIn, traffic and emailing, with cost per qualified lead and per signature.
- **Sales**: leads, pipeline and clients, including time to first contact and to kickoff.
- **Data**: quality checks, plus data you can edit, export and import as JSON.
