A project from the "Marketing stack and no-code tools" course, still in progress. It is my first chatbot.

## The idea

Help someone who does not know which health professional to turn to: they describe what they are looking for, and the service points them in the right direction. The site offers two paths:

- **fill in a request**: first name, last name, email, phone and postcode, with explicit consent;
- **chat directly with a chatbot**, which asks the questions and guides the search.

## What the site shows

- a three-step journey (describe your need, options are reviewed, you get guidance);
- example specialties (GP, cardiologist, paediatrician, other carers);
- the service's commitments (listening, clarity, human support, privacy) and an FAQ, which makes clear the service is neither a diagnosis nor an emergency line (15 or 112 in France).

## How it was built

The first mockup was designed with Claude under the name "Médilink". The chatbot and request handling run on **n8n**. The mockup's testimonials and figures are fictional.
